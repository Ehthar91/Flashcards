FLASHCARDS AUTHENTICATION ISOLATION

When Flashcards is opened inside Classroom Tools, the iframe now loads:
  flashcards.html?embedded=classroom

Embedded Flashcards uses a named Firebase app with in-memory authentication.
This means:
- It does NOT restore a previous standalone Flashcards Google login.
- Teachers must click Continue with Google inside the embedded Flashcards tool.
- Signing in/out of embedded Flashcards does not sign the standalone Flashcards page in/out.
- The embedded Flashcards login lasts only for the current loaded page/iframe session.
- The standalone Flashcards page keeps its normal remembered Firebase login.
- Classroom Tools' This device / Google account setting remains independent.

No Firebase rules change is required for this update.
